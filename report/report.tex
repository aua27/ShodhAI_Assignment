\section{Formulation}
\textbf{Model.} $u_t=\partial_x[a(x)u_x]$, $a=a_0e^{\beta(2x-1)}$, $u(0,t)=g(t)$,
$-a(1)u_x(1,t)=h[u(1,t)-T_a(t)]$; readings $y=u(x_s,t)+b_s+\varepsilon$, $b_{\rm ref}=0$.
$a_0$ is the mid-rod conductivity, $e^{2\beta}$ the right/left ratio; a heater clamps $x{=}0$ and Newton
cooling acts at $x{=}1$. Heat spreads only $\sqrt{at}\approx0.3$--$0.4$ in an experiment, so only the
right-most sensors see the Robin end: $h$ is expected to be the hardest parameter, and it trades off with
conduction and offsets.

\textbf{Inverse problem.} $\theta=(a_0,\beta,h,b_s)$ within the brief's bounds; minimise
$\tfrac12\sum_k[(u_k(\theta)+b_{s(k)}-y_k)/\sigma_k]^2$, the Gaussian likelihood with the supplied noise sd.
Offsets are persistent per sensor and enter linearly; the reference sensor fixes the level.
Uncertainty: Laplace covariance $(J^\top J)^{-1}$, 95\% Wald intervals (local, conditional on the model).
Assumptions --- independent Gaussian noise with known sd, correct model form, piecewise-linear inputs --- are
each tested by a guard (\S5).

\textbf{Data.} All data are synthetic, generated to the brief's schema with
truth kept in a separate file read only for scoring; results on them are marked\syn. Cleaning is explicit
and logged per row: \ClnBad\ \texttt{bad}, \ClnConflict\ conflicting repeated-ID (all copies), \ClnDup\
duplicate, \ClnMissing\ missing and \ClnTime\ out-of-domain rows dropped; \ClnSuspect\ \texttt{suspect}
kept. Fit E1--E3, validate E4 (labels never reach fitting), predict E5 from its inputs only. Methods were
frozen at \texttt{\FreezeCommit}; estimates are \RunsIdentical\ across the three evaluation runs.

\section{Stage 1 --- reference solver and inference}
Cell-centred finite volumes (conservative), Robin end as half-cell conduction in series with $h$,
Crank--Nicolson with Rannacher start-up. \textbf{Verification}, independent of the inverse code:
manufactured solution, order \OrdMin--\OrdMax\ (Fig.~\ref{fig:ver}a), RMSE \MmsErrHundred\ at the production
grid ($100\times400$) vs noise $\approx10^{-2}$; closed-form steady state \SteadyErr; energy balance
\EnergyDefect; maximum principle. Data are generated on a $4\times$ finer grid (no inverse crime).
\textbf{Fitting:} bounded trust-region least squares, 4 Latin-hypercube starts. Known-parameter recovery is
tested on separate synthetic data; coverage over 20 datasets is in \S4.

($\chi^2_\nu{=}\FvChi$). The constant baseline is the cautionary case: almost the same error against noisy

\section{Stage 2 --- inverse PINN}
\textbf{Design.} One $4{\times}48$ tanh MLP per training experiment (smooth second derivatives; each
experiment has its own forcing, the physics is shared); $a_0,\beta,h$ bounded by sigmoids, offsets by
$0.03\tanh$. Losses $L_{\rm obs},L_{\rm pde},L_{\rm ic},L_{\rm bc}$ are in noise units (divided by the median
sensor variance, weights 1), so violating physics by one noise level costs about one misfitted reading.
$L_{\rm pde}$ uses the conservative operator by autograd: flux $=a(x)u_x$, then $\partial_x$flux, so
$a'(x)u_x$ appears automatically (tested against the hand-derived source to $10^{-10}$). 2000 uniform
collocation points per experiment, resampled every 250 Adam steps; then L-BFGS on a fixed set. Physics is
frozen for 1500 warm-up steps. Unseen experiments: physics-only adaptation, \PinnAdaptSec\,s/seed.

\textbf{Development} (separate dev bundle, one change per trial, \texttt{docs/evidence}): no warm-up
$\to$ $h$ collapsed onto its bound; warm-up $\to$ $a_0,\beta$ right, $h$ $3\times$ low; $w_{\rm bc}{=}10$
$\to$ ``BC weight pins $h$'' refuted; more steps marginal; $w_{\rm pde}{=}10$ worse. Mechanism: soft
constraints leave about one noise level of slack between the last sensor ($x{=}0.85$) and the Robin end,
enough for the field to bend so a wrong $h$ satisfies the boundary condition. Tuning stopped there.

\textbf{Result.} The PINN fits the readings (train RMSE \PinnTrainRmse) but its parameters are off: $h$ by
\PinnErrH\%, $a_0$ by \PinnErrA\%; only $\beta$ agrees with FV. Its seed spread is not uncertainty, and
agreement with an FV field at its own parameters (RMSE \PinnCons) is consistency, not accuracy. An
exact-physics likelihood-ratio check places every seed outside the FV fit's 99.9\% region
($\Delta\chi^2$ \PinnDchiMin--\PinnDchiMax\ vs \PinnDchiLim), so all are reported \texttt{weak\_evidence}.
The PINN does not beat the numerical baseline here; FV is primary.

\section{Stage 3 --- investigation}
\textbf{Uncertainty (Fig.~\ref{fig:ver}b).} Coverage of 95\% intervals on \CovIndN\ independent synthetic
datasets: \CovIndA, \CovIndB, \CovIndH\ of \CovIndN\ for $a_0,\beta,h$ (Wilson lower bound
\CovIndWilsonLo; small $n$, a sanity check). Data uncertainty alone (20 noise redraws): empirical/Laplace sd
\RedrawRatioA, \RedrawRatioB, \RedrawRatioH\ --- mildly optimistic, not conclusive at $n{=}20$. Seed
variability is separate: zero for FV (multistarts agree), the Table~\ref{tab:main} spread for the PINN.
With AR(1) noise ($\rho{=}0.6$) plain intervals cover \CovArRawA, \CovArRawB, \CovArRawH\ of \CovArN; the
guard flags all \CovArFlagged\ and widens by $(1{+}\hat\rho)/(1{-}\hat\rho)$, restoring \CovArA, \CovArB,
\CovArH; it flags \CovIndFlagged\ independent-noise datasets.

\textbf{Controlled experiments (Fig.~\ref{fig:abl}).} One change each, hypotheses stated in code beforehand,
fixed evaluation (held-out latent RMSE). Removing sensors at $x\ge0.7$ or keeping only $t\le0.3$ widens
the $h$ interval $\AbHWidthRatioRight\times$; early times also widen $\beta$; dropping left sensors mainly
widens $\beta$. Assuming constant $a$ gives $\AbLatRatioConst\times$ the held-out latent error; forcing
offsets to zero pushes $\beta$ to its bound (\AbZeroOffB).

\textbf{Experimental design.} A new sensor at $x{=}0.95$ vs the same 24 readings per experiment on existing
sensors. Rule, fixed before truth: pick the design with the smaller predicted sd of $h$ at a pilot fit.
It chose the new sensor in \DesRuleA/\DesN\ datasets and narrowed the $h$ interval in \DesAWins/\DesN\ (width
\DesAHW\ vs \DesBHW), but $|h|$ error did not measurably differ (\DesAErr\ vs \DesBErr): a precision gain,
not a demonstrated accuracy gain.

\textbf{The three questions.} \emph{Can bias mask wrong physics?} Yes: with $\beta{=}0.45$ the constant-$a$
model fits at $\chi^2_\nu{=}\AbConstAChi$ (limit \AbConstALim, accepted) with $\AbLatRatioConst\times$ the
latent error; only model comparison or held-out prediction exposes it. \emph{Which parameters become weak?}
$h$ first (right-end or late data removed), then $\beta$ (early times); $a_0$ stays determined.
\emph{Which measurement helps?} A sensor near the Robin end: it sees the boundary gradient directly and
breaks the $h$/conduction/offset trade-off.

\section{Stage 4 --- reliability, diagnosis, recovery}
\textbf{Automatic status.} Every result is \texttt{ok}, \texttt{weak\_evidence} or \texttt{failed} from rules
in code that never use truth: $\chi^2_\nu>1{+}3\sqrt{2/\nu}$; positive residual autocorrelation ($z{>}3$);
held-out predictive $\chi^2$; nested test constant vs variable $a$ ($p{=}10^{-3}$); interval wider than
50\% of the range $\Rightarrow$ estimate withheld; estimate on a bound; multistart disagreement; PINN
exact-physics check; fault scan. False-alarm rates were measured on correct models.

\textbf{Diagnosis.} Rules frozen in \texttt{configs/diagnosis\_rules.yaml}. Observations (residual trends,
$\chi^2$, early/late parameter shift) are reported separately from causal hypotheses, each a one-parameter
extension: drift of each sensor, a Gaussian heat source (centre scanned), a bias in the recorded $g$.
Evidence is the $\chi^2$ drop; the extra parameter $\pm$ sd is the sensitivity. Threshold 15.1 (Bonferroni,
10 hypotheses, family $p{=}10^{-3}$); on \NullN\ fault-free bundles the largest drop is \NullMax\
(\NullFalse\ false alarms); \DiagValOk/\DiagValN\ known faults correct; fault-size sweep \SweepDetected/\SweepN\
detected, \SweepNamedWrong\ named wrongly. Undetected small faults still bias $\beta$ by up to
\SweepMissBetaErr. \textbf{Blind test:} rules committed, then a sealed bundle with a randomly drawn fault and
its truth (hash in the manifest), then the verdict, then unsealing; re-running with truth reads blocked
gives the same verdict. Verdict \emph{\SealVerdict} ($\Delta\chi^2{=}\SealDchi$, $Q{=}\SealQ\pm\SealQsd$);
truth: heat source, $Q{=}\SealTrueQ$ at $x{=}\SealTrueXc$ (integrated heat within \SealHeatRatio\%). The base fit
there said \texttt{ok} with $h{=}\SealBaseH$ (true \SealTrueH), so the fault scan now runs inside
\texttt{evaluate} (public bundle: \ScanVerdict).

\textbf{Recovery and visibility.} Checkpoints carry a fingerprint (config + data hash). A run killed
mid-training and resumed matches the uninterrupted run exactly (difference \RestartDiff); a checkpoint
from another config is refused; a truncated file is skipped; an injected non-finite loss is logged,
rolled back and the learning rate halved. Every result logs data hash, config, seed, git commit, hardware,
versions and wall time.

\textbf{A misleading result.} In the first evaluation a PINN seed passed the old check with
$h{=}\VOneSeedTwoH$ (true \TrueH): $\chi^2_\nu{=}\VOneSeedTwoChi$ vs limit \VOneSeedTwoLim. A wrong $h$ moves
$\chi^2_\nu$ by only \VOneChiGap, so an absolute test has little power; the replacement compares against
the FV optimum on the same data and rejects it clearly. Testing also found six input-handling bugs, all fixed.

\textbf{Compute.} Laptop i7-1165G7, 16\,GB, no GPU: FV fit \FvFitSec\,s; PINN \PinnTrainSec\,s/seed +
\PinnAdaptSec\,s adaptation; public evaluation \EvalWall\,min; CPU smoke run \SmokeSec\,s (\SmokeOk, budget
10\,min) on a 2-vCPU cloud runner; \NTests\ tests pass.

\section{Claims, limitations, time}
\textbf{Supported\syn:} FV recovery with calibrated intervals; $h$ is the weakly identified parameter and a
Robin-end sensor improves its precision; guards detect dependent noise, wrong diffusivity form, PINN
parameter inconsistency and three fault types above measured sizes.
\textbf{Not supported:} anything on real data; faults below detection bias $\beta$; Laplace intervals are
local; the PINN's $h$; design accuracy gain; coverage from only 20 datasets.
\textbf{Time spent:} $\approx$4\,h build, experiments and report (24 Oct 21:56 -- 25 Oct 02:07 IST);
25 Oct: cloud smoke run, fact-check of every number and fixes; author review and walkthrough preparation.

\section*{Appendix --- AI assistance and verification}
\textbf{How AI was used.} Claude was used as a pair-programmer throughout: drafting code, running
experiments and drafting this report. I directed the plan, decisions and trade-offs; decisions such as
stopping PINN tuning, adding guards and tightening thresholds were discussed and approved explicitly.

\textbf{How the output was verified --- by evidence, not trust.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Independent references:} the solver against a manufactured solution, a closed-form steady
state, energy conservation and the maximum principle; the PINN operator against the same hand-derived source.
\item \emph{Known truth:} all studies use synthetic data with truth stored separately and read only for
scoring; a file guard proves fit/predict never open it; corrupting validation labels leaves training
estimates bit-identical.
\item \emph{Pre-registration:} diagnosis rules committed before the sealed blind test, methods before the
final evaluation; rule changes (one-sided autocorrelation test, Bonferroni threshold, stronger PINN check
after the first evaluation run) are declared with their evidence.
\item \emph{Adversarial testing:} \NTests\ tests (metamorphic shift/scale invariance, renamed IDs and layouts,
row order, edge-of-box truths, malformed inputs, CLI; autograd operator, Jacobian, decision branches). They
found six real bugs, one wrong test premise and a dependency-pin gap on a fresh clone.
\item \emph{AI errors caught:} an ad-hoc threshold, a guard with too little power, a premature claim that a
status rule was protective, a mis-stated clock time, and a figure drawing the superseded diagnosis
threshold. Each was challenged, re-measured and corrected.
\item \emph{No hand-typed results:} every number here is generated by \texttt{report/make\_numbers.py}
from the result files.
\end{itemize}
